Common Questions

Before You Book

Which areas do you serve?

We cover Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane, and nearby locations including Dombivli, Kalyan, Panvel, Vasai, Virar, and more.

How do I get a price quote?

WhatsApp us your event date, location, and a photo of the venue or theme you like. We reply with options and pricing — usually within a few hours.

How early should I book?

For weddings and big events, book 2–4 weeks ahead. For birthdays and smaller setups, 3–7 days is often enough — message us and we'll confirm availability.

What's included in your service?

Theme design, balloons, floral backdrops, stage decor, table styling, setup, and on-site coordination. We bring everything needed and leave the venue looking picture-perfect.
